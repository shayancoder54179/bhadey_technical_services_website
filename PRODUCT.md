# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Contractors, project managers, and facilities/construction firms across the UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and beyond) who need to verify what's underground or embedded before cutting, drilling, or excavating. Often evaluating under time pressure — a job is scheduled and a scan needs booking fast. They care about credibility (has this company worked on real projects like Dubai Mall, Emaar, ADNOC?) and equipment specifics (Proceq GP8000, depth/accuracy numbers).

## Product Purpose

Generate qualified contact (WhatsApp message, call, or quote form) from visitors researching GPR scanning, core cutting, excavation, or oversized cargo escort services. Success means a fast, low-friction path to contact, with enough technical and credibility signal that a contractor trusts this isn't a fly-by-night operation.

## Brand Personality

Modern, technical. Precise instrumentation credibility rather than generic corporate-services polish. Confident enough to look distinct from every other UAE contractor site.

## Anti-references

Generic navy-blue-and-orange corporate services template: dot-grid hero backgrounds, rounded stock icon cards, stacked-badge stat sections. Nothing that could be mistaken for a template theme.

## Design Principles

1. The product is "see what's hidden before you cut" — let that show up structurally (depth/cross-section motifs), not just as decoration.
2. Two-color discipline: black + the logo's orange only. No introduced third brand color.
3. Contact is always one tap away (WhatsApp/call), never buried behind navigation.
4. Technical specificity builds trust — real numbers (1m in concrete, 3–5m in soil, ±10mm, 12–500mm) over vague marketing language. Depth figures are equipment-specific: the GP8000 scans concrete to 1m, the GS8000 scans ground to 3–5m depending on soil. Never quote a single blended depth number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA — contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text. English-only for now.
